Reconnect Privacy Policy

Reconnect - Bluetooth device manager, by ByteAcre · Effective 13 October 2026

Reconnect is a Windows app that helps Bluetooth earbuds and headphones reconnect reliably. This policy explains what information the app uses and what it does with it. In short: Reconnect does not collect, send or share any of your data. Everything stays on your PC.

Information the app uses

  • Bluetooth devices. To find, pair and remove (forget) your Bluetooth devices, Reconnect reads the names and Bluetooth addresses of nearby and paired devices.
  • Audio volume. Reconnect reads and sets the Windows volume of your Bluetooth audio device so it can restore your preferred volume when the device reconnects.

Information stored on your PC

Reconnect saves the following only on your PC, in the app's own data folder:

  • the names and Bluetooth addresses of the devices you add, their last volume level, when they last connected, and your settings for each device;
  • a small log file of what the app did (for example, "connected" or "pairing failed"), which helps with troubleshooting. The log never leaves your PC unless you choose to send it to us.

You can delete a device and its saved settings at any time with the app's Remove button. Uninstalling the app removes its data folder.

What the app does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet or send any information to ByteAcre or anyone else.
  • It does not include advertising, analytics or tracking.
  • It does not access your files, contacts, location, microphone or camera.

Changes to Windows Bluetooth settings

For devices you add, Reconnect can remove (forget) the device from Windows Bluetooth after it disconnects, and pair it again when you click Connect. This is what fixes devices that otherwise won't reconnect. It is turned on per device and you can turn it off at any time. If you choose "Start with Windows", the app starts when you sign in; you can turn this off in the app or in Windows Settings.

Children

Reconnect does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
